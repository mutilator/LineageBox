Family members sign in with Google, or with a passkey: the lock of their own phone or computer, for those without a Google account. A member sees their own part of the family: their ancestors, every blood relative, the partners of those and their children, and their own partner's side; the photos those people are in; what they upload; and what others share with them. An admin sees and changes everything. What is shared lists everything shared, by whom and with whom.

Sign-in

On: everyone signs in, at home too, and the site can be opened from outside. Off: only the home network, with no sign-in, as before.

In Google Cloud → APIs & Services → Credentials, the client's authorised redirect URI is {{ redirect_uri }}. Passkeys are made for this name too: change it later, and everyone's passkeys stop working until they make new ones.

A passkey signs someone in with the lock of their phone or computer (their face, fingerprint or PIN) instead of Google: for those without a Google account, or anyone who would rather. To give someone one, open their account below, press Make a passkey link and send them the link: it works once, for {{ invite_days }} days ({{ admin_hours }} hours for an admin's account). Once signed in, they can add passkeys on their other devices from the round button at the top → Passkeys.

Passkeys need three things: HTTPS (a certificate, or a reverse proxy that does HTTPS: see Address and HTTPS); the Address from outside above, a name such as photos.example.com; and that name listed exactly under Host names, with no name with * there. Browsers only use a passkey on the one name it was made for, over HTTPS with a certificate they trust.
