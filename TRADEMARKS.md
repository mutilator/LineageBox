# The LineageBox name

LineageBox's code is free software under the [GNU Affero General Public License, version 3](LICENSE): anyone may use,
change and share it. The licence covers the code, not the name. "LineageBox" is a trademark of Scott Gusler, so that
people can tell the project, and copies that are the same as it, from changed ones.

## Fine without asking

- Saying what is true: that something works with LineageBox, is a guide or an add-on for it, or is based on it.
- Sharing LineageBox unchanged, as the project releases it (its images, its source code), under its name.
- Writing about LineageBox or showing it: articles, videos, talks, reviews.
- Changing your own copy and keeping the name, for you and your family.

## Use another name

- **For a changed copy you share or offer to others** (a fork, an image or package of it, a hosted service): pick a
  name that can't be mistaken for LineageBox, and use it wherever people see the name: the pages, the image and
  package names, your site. You may say it is "based on LineageBox". Names inside the code (the `lineagebox` package,
  the `LINEAGEBOX_*` settings) can stay.
- **For a product, service, company or domain name** that could make people think it is LineageBox, or that it comes
  from or is backed by the project.

A fork still follows the licence: keep the copyright notices, including the one at the end of the help panel, and
offer the source code of your copy to the people who use it.

Not sure whether your use is fine, or want permission for something else? Ask in an issue on the project's GitHub page.
